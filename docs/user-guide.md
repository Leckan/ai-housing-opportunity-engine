# AHOE demo user guide

## Start with Overview

The overview summarizes the synthetic parcel set, score distribution, and highest-ranked sites. The banner on every data-heavy area explains that values are generated and unverified.

## Find and inspect a site

Open **Housing Opportunity Map**, search a demonstration label, APN, city, or category, and select a generated shape or result row. Use **Property Intelligence** to review the record, sample zoning label, preliminary capacity, constraints, or score factors. Save a site to keep it in **Saved Properties** in this browser.

## Explore zoning

Open **Zoning Intelligence** and enter a question or choose a prompt. Responses cite fictional sample excerpts to show the intended source-review experience. The answers do not establish current adopted regulations.

## Run a feasibility scenario

Open **Development Feasibility**, change any assumption, then run the calculation. Unit count is the lower of the assumed density capacity and net residential floor area capacity. Hard costs use construction cost per square foot; soft costs are 22% of hard costs, contingency is 8%, and financing is 7% of the hard, soft, and contingency subtotal. NOI is 62% of gross rent. These fixed ratios are demonstration assumptions.

## Compare affordability and rankings

Use **Affordable Housing** to change total units, set-aside share, illustrative market rent, and a simplified income band. **Opportunity Rankings** lets you sort by score, capacity, or area and filter by minimum score. Neither feature uses protected characteristics.

## Ask and export

The **AI Housing Assistant** answers using deterministic demo logic over the current synthetic data. **Reports** downloads a JSON or CSV property brief and a CSV ranking. Each export labels its data classification and limitations.

## Workspace and administration screens

Project, team, notification, and administration screens demonstrate the planned information architecture. Invite actions are staged locally; no email is sent. User roles are display-only and are not enforced by the local API.
